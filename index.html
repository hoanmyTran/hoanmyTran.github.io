01 / ABOUT

A little about me.

Background, academic path, and the questions that drive my research.

I am Hoan My Tran, a lecturer and researcher at IUT de Lannion, University of Rennes, France. I defended my Ph.D. in Computer Science in March 2026 at the University of Rennes, within the IRISA / EXPRESSION research team.

My doctoral research was supervised by Prof. Damien Lolive, Prof. François Goasdoué, Prof. Pierre-François Marteau, Asst. Prof. Arnaud Delhay-Lorrain, and Asst. Prof. Aghilas Sini.

Previously, I studied computer science at IUT de Villetaneuse (Sorbonne Paris North University) and earned an engineering degree specializing in software engineering from IMT Atlantique.

Beyond research, I enjoy communicating science to wider audiences. I was invited to give a TEDx talk ↗ about deepfake technology and its societal impact.
